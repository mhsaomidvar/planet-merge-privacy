Privacy Policy

Planet Merge by Lazy Fennec · Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Planet Merge ("the app", "the game"), published by Lazy Fennec ("we", "us"), handles information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. The game does not require an account, and it does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your best score and your settings (music, sound effects, and vibration) locally on your device so they are remembered between sessions. This data stays on your device, is not sent to us or to anyone else, and is removed when you uninstall the game.

Advertising and analytics

This version of the game does not show advertisements and does not use third-party analytics or advertising services.

Third-party links

The game may contain links to external pages, such as our social media page. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their content or practices.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the game does not collect personal data, no such information is gathered from users of any age.

Changes to this policy

If we add features that change how information is handled (for example, advertising), we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above before releasing that version.
